# SABI AI

**Speak Freely. Understand Instantly.**

SABI AI is an AI-powered multilingual communication platform being built to help people communicate across language barriers through a unified meeting experience.

## What SABI AI is building

SABI combines multilingual meetings with AI-powered communication capabilities, including:

- Real-time speech translation
- Speech-to-text transcription
- Language selection
- Meeting creation and joining
- Meeting scheduling with timezone and duration
- In-meeting chat
- Meeting history

The product is being developed first with African business communication needs in mind, with a long-term opportunity to serve global teams and organizations.

## Product workflow

```
Create Meeting
     ↓
Choose Languages
     ↓
Join Meeting
     ↓
Speak Naturally
     ↓
AI Speech Processing
     ↓
Translation + Transcription
     ↓
Review Meeting History
```

## Current stage

SABI AI is currently at the **MVP / early validation / pre-revenue** stage.

The MVP includes a multilingual meeting workflow with translation, transcription, scheduling, chat, and meeting history. The product is being tested and iterated before broader B2B pilots and paid deployment.

## Technical direction

SABI is designed around a modular AI communication pipeline:

```
Audio Input
    ↓
Speech Recognition
    ↓
Language Processing
    ↓
Translation
    ↓
Translated / Transcribed Output
```

The architecture can integrate external AI services for speech recognition, translation, and related language-processing tasks while the SABI product layer handles meeting workflows, user experience, orchestration, and communication.

## Why SABI

Multilingual communication often requires people to combine separate meeting, translation, transcription, scheduling, and documentation workflows.

SABI's product direction is to bring these capabilities into one communication experience.
