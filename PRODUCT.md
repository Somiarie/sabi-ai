# Product Overview

## Core experience

SABI AI is designed around a simple flow:

**Create → Connect → Speak → Translate → Transcribe → Review**

### Create

A host creates a meeting and can configure:

- Date
- Time
- Timezone
- Duration
- Languages
- Participants

### Connect

Participants join the meeting through a dedicated meeting environment.

### Speak

Participants communicate using their preferred language.

### Translate

SABI processes speech and provides translated communication.

### Transcribe

The meeting can generate text from spoken communication.

### Review

Meeting history provides a place to return to previous meeting records.

## Current MVP capabilities

The current product direction includes:

- Meeting creation
- Meeting joining
- Language selection
- Real-time translation
- Transcription
- Meeting scheduling
- In-meeting chat
- Meeting history

## Product principles

### Simplicity
Users should not need to understand the underlying AI infrastructure to communicate.

### Reliability
Real-time communication requires predictable audio and processing.

### Accessibility
The product should reduce language-related friction rather than create another complicated workflow.

### Global usability
Although SABI is being developed from an African context, the product is intended to address a global communication problem.
